
=== requirements_01_pricing.txt ===

PRICING AND DISCOUNTS (Section 1)

Members receive a 10% discount on eligible pricing rules when is_member is True.
Non-members pay the standard computed amount with no member discount.
Negative quantity must be rejected with ValueError on every pricing_rule function.
Zero quantity must return 0.0 for pricing rules that use the zero-quantity branch.
Bulk discount applies when quantity exceeds 50 units (BULK_DISCOUNT_THRESHOLD).
Premium flag may apply an additional reduction when combined with membership.
All monetary results must be rounded to two decimal places.
compute_pricing_bundle must sum the first ten pricing rules for a given quantity.
pricing_rule functions must not accept negative quantity.

=== requirements_02_inventory.txt ===

INVENTORY (Section 2)

Inventory rules must validate quantity is non-negative before calculation.
inventory_rule functions follow the same membership and premium semantics as pricing.
Quantity zero must yield zero total where the rule defines an early return.
Rules that require at least one unit must raise ValueError when quantity is below 1.
compute_inventory_bundle aggregates the first ten inventory rules.
Inventory calculations must use _round_money for final values.
Tiered logic (standard, silver, gold) must apply correct multipliers by quantity band.
Member tier rules apply an extra 0.02 multiplier reduction when is_member is True.

=== requirements_03_shipping.txt ===

SHIPPING AND FULFILLMENT (Section 3)

Shipping rules must reject negative quantity via _reject_negative.
Loop-based shipping rules cap the iteration count at five steps maximum.
Member shipping adjustments must not produce negative totals (floor at zero).
Premium shipping adds fee components before membership halving where applicable.
compute_shipping_bundle must return the sum of shipping_rule_001 through shipping_rule_010.
Shipping fee base of 2.5 may increase by 1.0 when premium is True.
All shipping outputs are expressed as floats rounded to two decimals.

=== requirements_04_payment.txt ===

PAYMENTS (Section 4)

Payment rules must raise ValueError for negative quantity.
Payment tier multipliers must match standard 1.0, silver 0.97, and gold 0.94 bands.
Members receive an additional 0.02 reduction on the tier multiplier.
compute_payment_bundle combines the first ten payment rules.
Payment results must never return unrounded long floats (use _round_money).
Quantity at or above 100 must classify as gold tier unless overridden by rule variant.

=== requirements_05_loyalty.txt ===

LOYALTY AND PROMOTIONS (Section 5)

Loyalty rules honor the global MEMBER_DISCOUNT constant for member paths.
loyalty_rule functions share bulk, tier, loop, and fee patterns with other domains.
compute_loyalty_bundle is the integration entry point for loyalty coverage.
Premium and is_member may be combined; order of application must match code.
Negative quantity is always invalid across loyalty_rule functions.
Zero quantity paths must return 0.0 where defined in the rule body.

=== requirements_06_returns_validation.txt ===

RETURNS, VALIDATION, AND INTEGRATION (Section 6)

returns_rule functions must follow the same validation and rounding conventions.
compute_returns_bundle sums the first ten returns rules.
validate_cart_line must reject empty SKU, negative price, negative quantity, and quantity above MAX_LINE_QTY.
validate_member_flag must raise TypeError when is_member is not a boolean.
combine_domain_bundles must call all six compute_*_bundle functions and return rounded total.
estimate_order_total returns 0.0 for zero quantity.
estimate_order_total applies a 20 currency unit member discount when total exceeds 500.
Integration functions must import and execute without side effects beyond calculation.
