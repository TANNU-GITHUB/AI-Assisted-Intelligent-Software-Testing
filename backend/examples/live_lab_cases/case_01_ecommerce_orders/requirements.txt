Members receive a 10% discount on each line item before tax is calculated.
Non-members pay full item price before tax.
Sales tax of 7.5% applies to the taxable subtotal after discounts.
Orders with a post-tax total strictly greater than 500 receive an additional 20 currency units off for members only.
Empty carts must produce a checkout total of exactly zero.
Negative unit prices must be rejected with ValueError.
Zero quantity line items must be rejected with ValueError.
Each line item must have a non-empty product SKU string.
Maximum 100 line items per order.
Shipping is flat 15 currency units unless the post-discount subtotal exceeds 250, then shipping is free.
Gift wrap adds 5 currency units per wrapped line.
Coupon codes starting with SAVE apply a flat 10 currency units off the order once per checkout.
Invalid coupon codes must not change the total.
Loyalty points redeem at 100 points equals 1 currency unit; redemption cannot exceed half the order subtotal.
Point balances cannot go negative after redemption.
Refunds credit the original payment method proportionally to line quantities returned.
Partial returns cannot exceed the original purchased quantity per SKU.
Order status must transition from draft to confirmed only when total is non-negative and at least one line exists.
Cancelled orders zero out totals but retain line history for audit.
High-value carts containing any single item priced above 1000 require manager approval (approval flag not in source — traceability gap).
International orders must collect harmonized sales tax tables (not implemented in source).
Email receipts must be sent within 60 seconds of confirmation (not implemented in source).
Inventory reservation must occur at confirm time (not implemented in source).
Promotional stacking: member discount applies before coupons, never after tax.
Reporting API must expose subtotal, tax, shipping, and loyalty components separately.
