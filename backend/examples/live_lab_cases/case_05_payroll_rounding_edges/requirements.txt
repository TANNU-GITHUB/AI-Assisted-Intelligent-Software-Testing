Regular hours are paid at the base hourly rate for each hour up to and including 40 hours per week.
Overtime hours are hours worked strictly beyond 40 per week, paid at 1.5 times the base rate.
All monetary amounts must use banker's rounding (round half to even) to two decimal places.
Gross pay equals regular pay plus overtime pay minus deductions.
Deductions cannot reduce net pay below zero.
Employees with zero hours worked receive zero gross pay.
Negative hours or negative rates raise ValueError.
Bonus is a flat 100 currency units when hours worked is at least 45 in a week.
Minimum wage floor: net pay after statutory deduction must not fall below 500 for full-time (>=40 hours).
Statutory deduction is 10% of gross unless gross is zero.
Part-time employees (under 40 hours) do not receive the bonus.
Time and a half applies to each overtime hour; overtime fraction hours are allowed.
Employer tax is 6.2% of gross and is not deducted from employee net (reporting only).
At exactly 40.0 hours, all pay is regular rate with zero overtime.
At 40.5 hours, 0.5 hours must be overtime at 1.5x.
At 41.0 hours, 1.0 hour must be overtime at 1.5x (not 0.0 overtime).
Banker's rounding: 2.005 rounds to 2.00, 2.015 rounds to 2.02.
Payslip net must never exceed gross.
Weekly batch totals must equal sum of individual payslip gross values within one cent tolerance.
