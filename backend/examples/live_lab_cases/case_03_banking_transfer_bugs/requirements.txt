Accounts must never allow a balance below zero after any withdrawal or transfer.
Transfer amounts must be strictly positive.
Duplicate transfer IDs in the same calendar day must be rejected.
Daily outgoing transfer sum per account must not exceed 10,000 currency units.
Withdrawals require sufficient available balance including pending holds.
Pending holds reduce available balance until released or captured.
Releasing a hold restores available balance without changing ledger balance.
Capturing a hold moves funds out and reduces both ledger and available balance.
Internal transfers between two accounts must debit and credit the same amount atomically.
External outbound transfers charge a flat 2 currency unit fee deducted from sender.
External inbound transfers have no fee.
Account IDs must be non-empty strings of at least 6 characters.
Transfer IDs must be unique globally for the lifetime of the system.
Negative transfer amounts raise ValueError.
Zero amount transfers raise ValueError.
Self-transfers to the same account ID are forbidden.
Currency is always USD; multi-currency is out of scope but amounts must round to cents.
Audit log must record every state change (not implemented in source — requirement for traceability gap).
